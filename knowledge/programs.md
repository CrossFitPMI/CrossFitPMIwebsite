# Sweat
Programmed with CrossFit-style movements, we’ve taken out the Olympic-lifting and the high skill Gymnastics so that any fitness level can join us in this class. There are no prescribed weights, so you can dial up or down the load as heavy or light as you need.

‍

We realise some athletes participate in sports or activities where lifting heavy weights do not translate to their needs or others who may have prior injuries that just don’t allow them to do so. We also realise there are many athletes no longer seeing results with their current Bootcamp, spin, or fitness class and want to try something new. We’ve created SWEAT keeping those ideas in mind.

‍

What to expect
A certified coach will guide you through warming up, followed by movement instruction and demonstration for the workout. The Coach will supervise and correct any movement performed by you during the workout.

‍

We scale each exercise to your fitness level, so you get the maximum workout every time.

‍

This program utilises functional movements. These movements, have analogs in nature and do a great job at doing a great deal of work quickly. We will squat, push, pull, jump, run, press, and throw as opposed to bicep curls and crunches. Taking slower, more enduring approach that is designed to build the broadest, general, inclusive fitness possible.

‍

Workouts can be adjusted in weight, intensity, and certain mechanics in order to scale to the experience and fitness levels of any individual, be it a total fitness beginner or a gym expert.

What is hyrox?
HYROX combines both running & functional workout stations, where participants run 1km, followed by 1 functional workout station, repeated eight times.

Ski Erg – 1,000m, Sled Push – 50m, Sled Pull – 50m, Burpee Broad Jumps – 80m, Row – 1,000m, Farmers Carry – 200m, Sandbag Lunges – 100m, Wall Balls – 100 reps.

‍

Each race is hosted indoors in expansive exhibition halls, creating an immersive and electrifying race, where your spectators can support you from the very beginning to the very end.

‍

This race format remains consistent across the globe, enabling global leaderboards & a cumulative World Championships at the end of each race season.

‍

Accommodating both professional athletes, and everyday fitness enthusiasts looking to take their training to the next level, HYROX is the sport for everybody.

‍

Are your Hyrox classes accessible?
Our HYROX classes are for everyone, no matter what their current fitness level.

‍

Whether you are looking to take on a HYROX race by yourself, share the experience with a teammate or just take part to increae your fitness, we can scale the class for anyone's abilty.

‍

What are the benefits of Hyrox?
HYROX is a unique blend of running and functional fitness that builds full-body strength, endurance, and mental resilience. The mix of steady running and challenging workout stations improves your cardiovascular fitness, boosts power and stamina, and helps you develop real-world functional strength. It’s suitable for all levels, highly measurable, and a great way to stay motivated by training for a structured, race-style event.

Olympic lifting provides benefits such as improved muscular power, coordination, and full-body strength, making it excellent for enhancing athletic performance in various sports. It also improves bone density, increases calorie burn, boosts metabolism, and promotes flexibility and balance.

‍

You will be paired with a coach in a small group class to refine your style through various accessory techniques.

‍

‍

Benefits for athletic performance
‍Enhances power and speed:

The explosive movements in Olympic lifting help improve rate of force development, which translates to better speed and power in sports like sprinting and jumping.

Builds coordination and balance:

The complex, full-body movements require precise coordination, rhythm, and timing, which improves overall balance and body awareness.

Improves agility and flexibility:

The dynamic nature of the lifts and the required range of motion can lead to better flexibility and agilit

‍

Benifits for health and fitness
Increases bone density:

The high forces placed on the skeleton stimulate the body to lay down new bone, which helps prevent osteoporosis and fractures.

Boosts metabolism:

As full-body, high-intensity movements, Olympic lifts burn more calories in a shorter amount of time compared to isolation exercises, helping with weight loss and fat reduction.

Builds full-body strength:

The lifts engage multiple large muscle groups simultaneously, including the legs, back, shoulders, and arms, leading to significant strength gains.

Improves work capacity:

The lifts can increase your capacity to do work by improving your cardiovascular endurance and muscular endurance.

Enhances mental well-being:

The process of learning and mastering the lifts can lead to increased confidence and provides an effective way to relieve stress
