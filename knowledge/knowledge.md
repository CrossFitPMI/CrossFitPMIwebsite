📍 Overview
CrossFit PMI is a CrossFit gym in Limerick, Ireland, and the site positions it as a coaching-led gym for people at different fitness levels.
The messaging focuses on expert coaching, supportive training, and helping members improve fitness through consistent habits and structured workouts.
The main conversion path across the site is a Free No-Sweat Intro.

💪 Products & Services
🏋️ CrossFit Classes - CrossFit Classes has its own dedicated program page and is presented as a core offering on the site.​
⚙️ Foundation Course - This program consists of 3 one-to-one sessions over a 1-week period designed to introduce new clients to the fundamental movements of CrossFit and how to scale them to their fitness level.
The page says these sessions take place every weekday at 7:30pm and are structured to help clients transition into regular classes after completion.
🚀 Get Started - This page describes the first visit as a gym tour plus a conversation about who they are, how they train, and what next steps fit the client’s goals, needs, and fitness level.​
It says the visit should take about an hour and is intended for both seasoned athletes and complete beginners.​
🔥 Sweat! - This program uses CrossFit-style movements but removes Olympic lifting and high-skill gymnastics so that any fitness level can join.​
The page says there are no prescribed weights, the coach guides warm-up and movement instruction, and each workout is scaled to the individual.​
It also frames the program as a fit for people who want something different from bootcamp, spin, or standard fitness classes, including those with injuries or different sport needs.​
🏃 Drop-In - The Drop-In page invites visitors who are in town or nearby to train at the gym as a temporary home away from home.​
The process is: sign up and pay for a pass, schedule the class, arrive a few minutes early, check in digitally, and introduce yourself to the coach before joining class.​
The page says visitors should have a minimum of 1 month of CrossFit experience.​
🥗 Nutrition Coaching - The nutrition page says fitness cannot be optimized without both exercise and nutrition and that the gym provides accountability and support to help clients see results.​
It describes the system as a simple habit-based approach with a customized plan aimed at long-term sustainable results.​
The page also frames nutrition as the foundation for performance and says food strongly affects how people look, feel, and perform.​
