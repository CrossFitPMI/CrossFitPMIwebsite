About Coach
I began my athletic journey in swimming, competing at a national level until the age of 15, a period during which I developed discipline and technique in the sport. Later, I joined my school's football team, which allowed me to experience teamwork and strengthen my collaborative skills. For the past five years, I have been practicing CrossFit, competing in the RX category, and solidifying my passion for functional training and high-performance sports.

Turning Point
There was a time in my life when I lost the motivation to continue with sports. The monotony, lack of results, and mental fatigue made me question if it was worth it. Regaining that motivation wasn’t easy, but I forced myself to reconnect with the reason behind my efforts, to redefine my goals, and to find ways to enjoy the process again. Changing up my routines and surrounding myself with inspiring people made all the difference. Little by little, I rediscovered the passion I thought I had lost, and it not only transformed my performance but also my life.

Motivation & Passion
I’m a coach who truly enjoys seeing the progress of my students. I’m passionate about helping them push their limits, both physically and mentally, and guiding them toward their goals. The progress they make, not only in their performance but also in their self-confidence, is what motivates me to continue growing as a coach.

About Coach
Micheál comes from an educational background and believes that education is key to improving people’s lives both physically and in turn mentally. From his perspective, there is an epidemic of marketing catchphrases in the fitness industry that need to be stamped out. He thinks words such as ‘Toning’, ‘Burning’, ‘Shredding’, etc., are all used in the wrong context and are trying to appeal to the ‘quick-fix’ mentality that resides in people today.

Turning Point
Micheál believes that a small amount of hard work and a shift in perspective can completely transform a person. He tries to educate people about what it really takes to reach their goals, whatever they may be. In each class he coaches he tries to teach people something new about their fitness/physical weakness/nutrition through the medium of CrossFit.

Motivation & Passion
For him, CrossFit opened his eyes as to what really needs to be done to get in better shape in a sustainable and lasting way. He thinks CrossFit has something for everyone, no matter what their background and is extremely satisfying and rewarding in the long run.
